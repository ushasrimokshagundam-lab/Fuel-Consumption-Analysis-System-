How mileage is calculated. Full-tank: distance between two consecutive full-tank fills ÷ all litres filled in that interval (accurate). Approximate: distance since the previous valid reading ÷ litres in a partial fill (only an estimate, because the tank level is unknown). Approximate values are labelled and excluded from averages unless you switch them on in the dashboard.
